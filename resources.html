Know Your Rights

Basic Legal Rights Every Citizen Should Know

General information only — please seek advice on your specific facts.

  • Right to know the grounds of arrest, and in bailable offences, the right to be told you may be released on bail.
  • Right to have a relative or friend informed of the arrest.
  • Right to meet an advocate during interrogation (though not throughout it).
  • Right to be produced before a Magistrate within 24 hours (excluding travel time).
  • Right to medical examination and to free legal aid.
  • Protection against self-incrimination — you cannot be compelled to be a witness against yourself (Article 20(3)).
  • Police must register an FIR for a cognizable offence. You are entitled to a free copy of the FIR.
  • Zero FIR: an FIR can be lodged at any police station irrespective of jurisdiction and transferred later.
  • Under the BNSS, information can also be given electronically (e-FIR), to be signed within the prescribed time.
  • If police refuse, you may approach the Superintendent / Commissioner of Police, or file an application before the Magistrate.
  • Statements of women in certain offences are to be recorded by a woman police officer, and as far as possible at her residence.
  • As a rule, women are not to be arrested after sunset and before sunrise except in exceptional circumstances with prior permission of the Magistrate.
  • Protection, residence and maintenance under the Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005.
  • Protection from sexual harassment at the workplace under the POSH Act, 2013.
  • Right to free legal aid regardless of income.
  • Rights to safety, information, choice, to be heard, to seek redressal and to consumer education.
  • Complaints can be filed where you reside or work — including online through e-Daakhil.
  • Generally to be filed within 2 years of the cause of action.
  1. Call 1930 immediately — the faster you report, the better the chance of freezing the money.
  2. Report on cybercrime.gov.in and note the acknowledgement number.
  3. Inform your bank and block cards / UPI.
  4. Preserve screenshots, transaction IDs, phone numbers and chat history — do not delete anything.
  5. Consult an advocate for FIR, recovery and court proceedings.
Under Section 12 of the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987, women, children, members of SC/ST communities, victims of trafficking, persons with disabilities, victims of disaster, industrial workmen, persons in custody, and persons below the notified income limit are entitled to free legal services. Contact the District Legal Services Authority (DLSA), Jalandhar, at the District Courts, or call 15100.

Be Prepared

Document Checklists

Bring copies of these to your consultation. Requirements may vary with facts and authority.

  • Copy of FIR
  • Arrest memo / custody details
  • Previous bail orders, if any
  • ID and address proof of accused
  • Surety's ID, address proof and property / FD documents
  • Medical records, if relevant
  • Details of other pending cases, if any
  • Marriage certificate / wedding photographs
  • Address proof of both spouses
  • ID proof and passport-size photographs
  • Proof of separate residence (one year or more)
  • Agreed terms — alimony, stridhan, custody
  • Income and asset details
  • Details of any pending cases between parties
  • Age proof of both parties (birth certificate / Class 10 certificate / passport)
  • Address proof of both parties
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Affidavit on marital status and age
  • Divorce decree / death certificate of previous spouse, if applicable
  • Three witnesses with ID proof
  • Notice of intended marriage (Special Marriage Act)
  • Title documents — chain of sale deeds
  • Latest Jamabandi / Fard and mutation (Intkal)
  • Approved building plan / CLU / NOC
  • Property tax receipts and utility bills
  • Encumbrance / no-dues certificates
  • ID and PAN of buyer and seller
  • Agreement to sell and payment proof
  • Death certificate
  • Family tree / list of legal heirs
  • ID and address proof of all heirs
  • Details of assets — bank accounts, FDs, shares, property
  • Will, if any
  • No-objection affidavits from other heirs, where applicable
  • Original dishonoured cheque
  • Bank return memo
  • Copy of legal notice and postal / courier receipts
  • Proof of delivery / tracking report
  • Documents proving the debt — agreement, invoice, ledger
  • Reply to notice, if received
  • FIR / DDR copy
  • MLC, medical records and bills
  • Post-mortem report and death certificate (in fatal cases)
  • Income proof of the injured / deceased
  • Age proof and ID of claimants
  • Vehicle registration, insurance and driving licence details
  • Photographs of the accident, if available
