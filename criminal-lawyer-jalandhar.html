Common questions

General information only. Your exact position depends on the facts of your case.

Jab kisi ko giraftari ka darr ho, to woh giraftari se pehle Sessions Court ya High Court mein anticipatory bail ki arzi laga sakta hai (Sec. 482 BNSS, pehle Sec. 438 CrPC). Court shartein laga kar giraftari se protection de sakti hai.
Ghabraayein nahi. Saare saboot (messages, recordings, documents) sambhal kar rakhein, anticipatory bail ke baare mein turant salah lein, senior police officers ko representation dein, aur zaroorat ho to High Court mein FIR quash karwane ki petition (Sec. 528 BNSS / Sec. 482 CrPC) laga sakte hain.
Likhit shikayat SSP / Police Commissioner ko bhejein. Phir bhi karwai na ho to Magistrate ke saamne arzi (Sec. 175(3) BNSS, pehle 156(3) CrPC) laga kar investigation ka order maanga ja sakta hai.
Ye offence, court aur case ke facts par depend karta hai. Bailable offence mein bail adhikar ke roop mein milti hai. Non-bailable offence mein court facts dekh kar faisla karti hai.
