Step by Step

The Bail process

Get the FIR copy

Check the sections, whether the offence is bailable, and the stage of the case.

Choose the right bail

Anticipatory bail (Sec. 482 BNSS) before arrest; regular bail (Sec. 480 / 483 BNSS) after arrest.

File in the right court

Magistrate, Sessions Court or High Court, depending on the offence and earlier orders.

Surety & bonds

Arrange a surety with ID and property / FD documents for the bail bonds.

Checklist

Documents usually needed

  • Copy of FIR
  • Arrest memo / custody details
  • Earlier bail orders, if any
  • ID and address proof of the accused
  • Surety's ID, address proof and property / FD papers
  • Medical records, if relevant
