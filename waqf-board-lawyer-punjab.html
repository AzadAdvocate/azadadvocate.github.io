Common questions

General information only. Your exact position depends on the facts of your case.

Jo property Islam ke tahat dharmik, pavitra ya charitable kaam ke liye hamesha ke liye dedicate ki jaati hai, jaise masjid, madrasa, qabristan, dargah ya unki aamdani wali property, use waqf property kehte hain.
Waqf property ke title, possession aur management ke zyadatar vivaad Waqf Tribunal mein jaate hain. Waqf Board ke orders ko High Court mein bhi challenge kiya ja sakta hai.
2025 ke amendment ne waqf properties ki registration, records aur Waqf Boards ke kaam karne ke tareeke mein kai badlaav kiye hain. Kuch provisions par court mein sunwai bhi chal rahi hai, isliye apni property ke baare mein taaza salah lein.
Waqf Board ko likhit shikayat dein aur Waqf Tribunal mein possession aur encroachment hatane ki karwai ki ja sakti hai. Saare records aur documents sambhal kar rakhein.
